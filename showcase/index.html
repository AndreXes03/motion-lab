SOUND

Movement has a voice.

Build procedural music and movement-driven 2D or 3D effects alongside the scene.

Sound design guide ↗

In supported browsers, the public Studio exports browser-rendered video at 720p or 1080p, PNG frames and JSON recipes. MP4 is available when the browser supports its encoder; otherwise it offers WebM. This uses the real-time Three.js/Canvas path. Cycles and file-based compositing run locally; mixed shots require prepared render inputs and a compositing plan.

FOR THE CURIOUS

A recipe you can read.

Scenes are parameterized code and JSON, sampled at an exact time. Motion curves can be grouped by camera or object, with one chosen shot window at a time. The source stays open when you want to go beyond the controls.
