FAIR QUESTIONS.

Before you
hit launch.

Can I make my founder video in the web demo?+

The public web demo is a lightweight way to explore Framevolve and its controls. The new founder setup is still a preview in development for the local Studio; it is not available in the web demo yet. Its first version uses your logo, screenshots, messages and call to action in an editable 24-second, widescreen 2D film.

Where does the AI part happen?+

For a custom video, you work with a coding assistant such as Codex or Claude to build the project from your brief and assets, then refine it in Framevolve. Framevolve does not include a one-click AI video generator. The upcoming guided founder setup provides a simpler template-based starting point.

Do I need to be a motion designer?+

You can start with a brief and use the focused controls to refine the result. The local Studio still needs installation, and a custom project can require help from your AI assistant. The founder workflow is being built to make that starting point simpler.

Is it actually free?+

Framevolve is free to use, including for professional work and selling your original rendered videos. An AI assistant, stock assets or other services may have separate costs. Software resale and repackaging have different terms; see the license.

Do I need references or perfect assets?+

A logo and clear product screenshots are a useful starting point. References are optional: share them with your assistant if they help explain the feel you want. You can begin with a short description of your audience, your product and your message.
