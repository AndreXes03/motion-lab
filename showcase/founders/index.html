FAIR QUESTIONS.

Before you
hit launch.

What can I try today?+

Explore the public demo and its controls. The founder setup is in development for local Studio: its first version makes an editable, 24-second widescreen 2D film from your logo, screenshots and messages. The films above are authored examples, not automatic founder-template outputs.

How does the AI part work?+

Bring your brief and assets to an assistant such as Codex or Claude. It helps build a custom project; you refine it in Framevolve. The Studio needs local installation. It does not include a one-click AI video generator.

Free, even for my startup?+

Yes. Framevolve is free for professional use and selling your original videos. Your AI assistant and other services may have their own costs. Software resale has separate license terms.
