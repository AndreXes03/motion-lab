FROM DOWNLOAD TO YOUR FIRST SESSION.

Take Framevolve
to your Mac.

The web demo is a limited preview. The full Studio runs locally on your Mac. Start with an internet connection and a folder you can keep and write to.

  1. Download and unzip.

    Download Framevolve · source ZIP ↓. Open the ZIP in Finder, then move the extracted motion-lab-main folder somewhere you want to keep it.

  2. Open Mac setup.

    Inside that folder, double-click Setup Framevolve.command. If asked, accept the private Node.js runtime download. Continue through the welcome screen, then choose Set up & open Framevolve. Keep the setup Terminal window open until Studio opens.

  3. Explore, then create.

    Preview, edit and export existing scenes in Studio. To create with AI, choose Create with AI or open the same folder in your coding assistant. Use your own provider account and access; any provider costs are separate.

This is source code with guided setup, not a signed or notarized app installer. If macOS blocks the command, or a step stops, follow the guide’s troubleshooting steps.

FAIR QUESTIONS.

Before you
hit launch.

What can I try today?+

Try the web demo for basic controls, or download and set up the full Studio on Mac. The founder creation flow is in development for local Studio: its first version makes an editable, 24-second widescreen 2D film from your logo, screenshots and messages. The films above are authored examples, not automatic founder-template outputs.

How does the AI part work?+

Use Codex or Claude Code with your own subscription, within your plan’s usage limits. Bring your brief and assets to the assistant: it helps build a custom project; you refine it in Framevolve. The Studio needs local installation. It does not include a one-click AI video generator.

Free, even for my startup?+

Yes. Framevolve is free for professional use and selling your original videos. Your AI assistant and other services may have their own costs. Software resale has separate license terms.
