## Bundles, plugins and dependencies

Include LICENSE and the legacy MIT notice with distributions and installers.
The same new contribution terms cover original Studio UI/editor work,
agent plugins/bridges, launchers, installers and update features. Third-party
packages and assets keep their original licenses; see [THIRD_PARTY.md](../THIRD_PARTY.md).
Do not label the whole distribution MIT in package metadata or marketing.

Flaticon Premium's attribution exception is conditional on the relevant download
during the subscription. The current icon provenance and verification status are
recorded in THIRD_PARTY.md. License/provenance files must be retained even if a
verified Premium download allows removal of the visible credit.
